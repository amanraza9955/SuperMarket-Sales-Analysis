# 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **SuperMarket Sales Analysis Dashboard** developed using **SQL, Python, and Power BI**. The project demonstrates a complete end-to-end data analytics workflow, beginning with raw sales data, followed by SQL-based business analysis, Python-powered data cleaning, and finally an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The dashboard helps business stakeholders monitor sales performance, compare branch and city performance, understand customer purchasing behavior, and identify high-performing product categories through dynamic visualizations.

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall supermarket sales performance.
- Compare sales across different cities and branches.
- Identify top-performing and underperforming product lines.
- Evaluate customer purchasing behavior.
- Analyze payment method preferences.
- Monitor monthly sales trends.
- Build an interactive dashboard for business reporting and decision-making.

# 📂 Dataset

The dataset contains supermarket sales transactions with the following information:

- Invoice ID
- Branch
- City
- Customer Type
- Gender
- Product Line
- Unit Price
- Quantity
- Tax
- Total Sales
- Payment Method
- Gross Income
- Rating
- Date & Time

The dataset was cleaned using **Python (Pandas)** before creating the dashboard in Power BI.

# 🔍 Key Business Insights

- 📍 Naypyitaw recorded the highest sales among all cities.
- 🍽️ Food & Beverages generated the highest revenue.
- 👥 Member customers contributed more revenue than normal customers.
- 👩 Female customers generated higher sales than male customers.
- 💳 Cash and E-wallet were the most preferred payment methods.
- 📅 January recorded the highest monthly sales.
- 🏪 Branch-wise analysis highlights opportunities to optimize inventory and improve operational performance.
